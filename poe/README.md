# Path of Exile 1 — SRS Necromancer (Standard)

A second-character plan for a player coming from Diablo II, after completing the
campaign once on Righteous Fire Chieftain. Class: **Witch → Necromancer**.
Skill: **Summon Raging Spirit (SRS)**. League: **Standard**.

> Knowledge current to ~January 2026 (patch 3.26 era). Numbers and transfigured-gem
> details drift between patches — cross-check anything specific against a current
> written guide. Recommended companion guide: **Ghazzy's SRS guide on Maxroll**
> (long-standing minion-build author, kept patch-current). Import his Path of
> Building and follow his tree directly for the authoritative version.

## Why this build

- One of the strongest, cheapest, most beginner-friendly setups in the game.
- Natural fit for a D2 Necromancer player.
- You level *as the real build* from Act 1 — no skill swap later.
- Active, satisfying playstyle (cast flaming skulls, they maul things), but still
  strong single-target for bosses, which many minion builds lack.
- Very tanky once ascended.
- On Standard you keep your whole stash and power-crept uniques are cheap, so it
  comes online fast.

## The big Diablo II → PoE differences

- **Resistances cap at 75%, and maps apply −30%** (worse with some mods). Before
  red maps (T11+) you want 75/75/75 fire/cold/lightning *after* that penalty —
  i.e. the character sheet shows ~105% before the penalty is visible. Chaos res:
  just push toward 0+, negative chaos res gets you killed by poison/Delirium.
- **Life is the whole game.** Stack max life on every gear slot and via the tree.
- **Flasks are active abilities**, not potions you hoard. You bind 5 and press
  them constantly: a life flask + utility flasks (granite for armour, quicksilver
  for movement, one for ailment immunity).
- **Install a loot filter now**: NeverSink via <https://filterblade.xyz>. Map
  drops are unreadable without one.
- **Currency orbs are crafting mats and money.** No gold. Chaos Orb and Divine
  Orb are the practical currencies.
- **Trading is via the website** (<https://pathofexile.com/trade>), then whisper
  the seller and party up. No in-game auction house.
- **Path of Building (Community fork)** is the offline planner everyone uses.
  Import your character by account + character name to see what each change does.
- **Get a movement skill** (Flame Dash for this build). Maps are big.

## Endgame structure (what comes after the campaign)

The campaign is the tutorial. The real game is the **Atlas of Worlds**: a second
passive tree plus ~120 zones ("maps") you run like an endless escalating Act.

1. Run maps; they drop higher-tier maps. Work T1 → T16.
2. Kill map bosses to unlock the **Atlas passive tree** (separate from the
   character tree, free to respec anytime).
3. Fight the **Conquerors** → **Sirus**, then **The Maven**, to earn
   **Voidstones**. Four Voidstones upgrades every map you run to max tier.
4. Then it's open-ended: farm currency, craft/buy gear, do pinnacle bosses
   (Shaper, Elder, Uber versions), or grind a league mechanic. No "you win" moment.

## Leveling — gems

You level as SRS from Act 1. Get these as they unlock:

| Gem | When | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Summon Raging Spirit** | First Witch skill reward | Cast constantly; skulls are temporary |
| **Minion Damage** (support) | Act 1 | |
| **Melee Physical Damage** (support) | Act 1–2 | |
| **Melee Splash** (support) | Act 1–2 | Pack clear |
| **Multistrike** (support) | Act 3 | More hits |
| **Summon Holy Relic** | Act 3, Library (buy from Siosa) | Attacks when your minions do and **heals you** — keep on bar all campaign |
| **Flame Dash** | Act 1–3 | Movement |
| **Flammability** | Act 1–3 | Curse; drops enemy fire res. Self-cast while leveling |
| **Clarity** (level 1–3 only) | Act 1 | Mana bridge; drop it later |
| **Infernal Legion** (support) | Optional | On the Holy Relic — burning AoE, melts packs while leveling |
| **Desecrate** + **Flesh Offering** (or Bone Offering) | When comfortable | Desecrate spawns corpses; Offering consumes them for a big minion buff. Becomes core |

Mana is the SRS pain point early — lean on Clarity (low level), a mana flask, and
a couple of mana/ES tree nodes until Offerings + ascendancy fix it.

## Leveling — gear rules

- Wand/sceptre + shield, or any weapon with **"+1 to Level of Socketed Gems"**.
- Rares with **life + resistances** on every slot.
- Cheap uniques that help: **Lifesprig** (wand), **Sidhebreath** (amulet),
  **Bones of Ullr** (boots), and a **Tabula Rasa** if you have/can grab one
  (6-link, trivial on Standard — put SRS in it).

## Ascendancy — Necromancer

Run **The Lord's Labyrinth** four times: normal → cruel → merciless → eternal.
Each is unlocked by completing that band's 6 Trials (you'll find most while doing
the campaign; check the map for missing ones).

Node order (flexible):

1. **Unnatural Strength** — +2 levels to all minion skill gems. Massive.
2. **Commander of Darkness** — your auras also give minions resistances +
   damage/cast speed.
3. **Bone Barrier** — energy shield, regen, phys damage reduction, scaling with
   active minions. Main defensive layer.
4. **Mistress of Sacrifice** — your Offerings also affect *you*, and last longer.

If mana is still miserable in early maps, take **Essence Glutton** before
Mistress — it refunds mana (and ES) off the corpses Desecrate makes.

## Passive tree

The tree barely matters during the campaign — just take life + a few minion nodes
+ mana sustain. The finished tree comes together around maps.

**How to execute:** the in-game tree has **Ctrl+F search** — type a notable's
name and it highlights with the shortest path. Or import Ghazzy's PoB and follow
it node-for-node. (A hand-written PoB URL can't be trusted — the encoding is a
node list.)

### While leveling — priority order

Roughly one theme per act; take the cheapest route to each notable.

- **Acts 1–2** (~first 20 pts) — minion damage, head toward life:
  `Lord of the Dead`, `Death Attunement`, plus the small minion/int nodes between.
- **Acts 3–4** — your life + mana:
  `Cruel Preparation` (life + chaos res), `Discipline and Training`, a mana/ES
  node or two. Run Clarity lvl 1–3.
- **Acts 5–7** — more minion power + life:
  `Grave Intentions` (minion chaos + ele res), `Gravepact`, `Constitution`.
- **Acts 8–10** — aura reservation + defense:
  `Sovereignty`, `Charisma` (reduced reservation — enables Determination + Anger
  + more), `Sanctuary` (spell block + ES), `Puppet Master`.

That's ~70–80 points and a functional mapping character.

### Finished target (~115 pts, level ~90)

- **Minion damage / speed:** `Lord of the Dead`, `Death Attunement`, `Gravepact`,
  `Puppet Master`, `Redemption`, `Spiritual Command` (if pathing lands there).
- **Minion survivability:** `Grave Intentions`, `Indomitable Army`.
  (Keeping minions alive keeps Bone Barrier strong and cuts resummoning.)
- **Your life** (aim ~4.5–5.5k): `Cruel Preparation`, `Constitution`,
  `Discipline and Training`, `Written in Blood`, `Sanctity`.
- **Your defense:** `Sanctuary`, `Prism Guard` (spell block). The minion wall is
  your first defensive layer, block is your second.
- **Aura reservation:** `Sovereignty`, `Charisma`, one reservation-efficiency node.
- **Optional:** `Whispers of Doom` (+1 curse — only if running two curses).

### Jewel sockets (grab 4–5; ignore during campaign)

- **Regular jewels:** roll for `increased Minion Damage`,
  `Minion Attack and Cast Speed`, `increased maximum Life`, and the conditional
  `Minions deal % increased Damage if you've used a Minion Skill recently`
  (always true for you).
- **1 Large Cluster Jewel** (Minion Damage, 8 passives): **Renewal** (minions
  deal more damage while you have an Offering) + **Feasting Fiends**.
- **1 Medium Cluster Jewel:** **Rotten Claws** (minion attack speed + maim) or
  **Vicious Bite** (minion crit).

## Endgame skill setup

**SRS 6-link (mapping):**
SRS + Minion Damage + Multistrike + Melee Physical Damage + Elemental Damage with
Attacks + Feeding Frenzy.

- **Bossing swap:** Feeding Frenzy → Concentrated Effect or Predator. Keep a
  separate 1-link Feeding Frenzy on another minion (Carrion Golem or Holy Relic)
  so you don't lose the buff.
- **Alternative:** transfigured **Summon Raging Spirit of Enormity** — fewer,
  bigger, longer-lasting spirits; more hands-off, great for bossing. Try base
  first, switch if you want less clicking.

**Auras (Necro runs a lot):** Determination + **Anger** (flat fire to minions —
staple) + **Hatred** or **Pride** + **Flesh and Stone**. Put **Generosity** on
Anger (boosts it for minions, costs you nothing).

**Curse:** Flammability, ideally via **Blasphemy** (aura) or a Curse-on-Hit setup
so it's automatic.

**Utility bar:** Desecrate + Flesh/Bone Offering, Convocation (pull + heal
minions), Flame Dash.

## Standard budget upgrades, in priority order

1. **6-link** for SRS — rare chest with life/minion mods, or start with a 6-link
   Tabula.
2. **+2 minion-gems Bone Helmet** with minion life (crafted or bought cheap).
3. **Helmet enchant:** "Summon Raging Spirit has +1 to maximum", or minion damage.
4. **+1 fire / +1 all spell gems amulet**, then **Ashes of the Stars**
   (very affordable on Standard) — +gem level and +quality to everything.
5. Rare wand crafted with **+1 all spell gems / +1 minion gems**, or a
   **Femurs of the Saints** staff (+2 support gems, minion attack/cast speed) as
   a cheap midgame stick.
6. **Watcher's Eye** with an Anger or Determination mod; **Cluster Jewels**
   (Renewal, Feasting Fiends); rare crafted Bone Helmet.
