== Path of Exile 1 — SRS Necromancer (Standard)

A second-character plan for a player coming from Diablo II, after completing the campaign once on Righteous Fire Chieftain. Class: *Witch → Necromancer*. Skill: *Summon Raging Spirit (SRS)*. League: *Standard*.

____
Knowledge current to ~January 2026 (patch 3.26 era). Numbers and transfigured-gem details drift between patches — cross-check anything specific against a current written guide. Recommended companion guide: *Ghazzy’s SRS guide on Maxroll* (long-standing minion-build author, kept patch-current). === Why this build

* One of the strongest, cheapest, most beginner-friendly setups in the game.
* Natural fit for a D2 Necromancer player.
* You level _as the real build_ from Act 1 — no skill swap later.
* Active, satisfying playstyle (cast flaming skulls, they maul things), but still strong single-target for bosses, which many minion builds lack.
* Very tanky once ascended.
* On Standard you keep your whole stash and power-crept uniques are cheap, so it comes online fast.

=== The big Diablo II → PoE differences

* *Resistances cap at 75%, and maps apply −30%* (worse with some mods). Before red maps (T11{plus}) you want 75/75/75 fire/cold/lightning _after_ that penalty — i.e. the character sheet shows ~105% before the penalty is visible. Chaos res: just push toward 0{plus}, negative chaos res gets you killed by poison/Delirium.
* *Life is the whole game.* Stack max life on every gear slot and via the tree.
* *Flasks are active abilities*, not potions you hoard. You bind 5 and press them constantly: a life flask {plus} utility flasks (granite for armour, quicksilver for movement, one for ailment immunity).
* *Install a loot filter now*: NeverSink via https://filterblade.xyz. Map drops are unreadable without one.
* *Currency orbs are crafting mats and money.* No gold. Chaos Orb and Divine Orb are the practical currencies.
* *Trading is via the website* (https://pathofexile.com/trade), then whisper the seller and party up. No in-game auction house.
* *Path of Building (Community fork)* is the offline planner everyone uses. Import your character by account {plus} character name to see what each change does.
* *Get a movement skill* (Flame Dash for this build). Maps are big.

'''''

=== Endgame structure (what comes after the campaign)

The campaign is the tutorial. The real game is the *Atlas of Worlds*: a second passive tree plus ~120 zones ("`maps`") you run like an endless escalating Act.

[arabic]
. Run maps; they drop higher-tier maps. Work T1 → T16.
. Kill map bosses to unlock the *Atlas passive tree* (separate from the character tree, free to respec anytime).
. Fight the *Conquerors* → *Sirus*, then *The Maven*, to earn *Voidstones*. Four Voidstones upgrades every map you run to max tier.
. Then it’s open-ended: farm currency, craft/buy gear, do pinnacle bosses (Shaper, Elder, Uber versions), or grind a league mechanic. No "`you win`" moment.

=== Leveling — gear rules

* Wand/sceptre {plus} shield, or any weapon with *"`{plus}1 to Level of Socketed Gems`"*.
* Rares with *life {plus} resistances* on every slot.
* Cheap uniques that help: *Lifesprig* (wand), *Sidhebreath* (amulet), *Bones of Ullr* (boots), and a *Tabula Rasa* if you have/can grab one (6-link, trivial on Standard — put SRS in it).

'''''

=== Ascendancy — Necromancer

Run *The Lord’s Labyrinth* four times: normal → cruel → merciless → eternal. Each is unlocked by completing that band’s 6 Trials (you’ll find most while doing the campaign; check the map for missing ones).

Node order (flexible):

[arabic]
. *Unnatural Strength* — {plus}2 levels to all minion skill gems. Massive.
. *Commander of Darkness* — your auras also give minions resistances {plus} damage/cast speed.
. *Bone Barrier* — energy shield, regen, phys damage reduction, scaling with active minions. Main defensive layer.
. *Mistress of Sacrifice* — your Offerings also affect _you_, and last longer.

If mana is still miserable in early maps, take *Essence Glutton* before Mistress — it refunds mana (and ES) off the corpses Desecrate makes.

'''''

=== Passive tree

The tree barely matters during the campaign — just take life {plus} a few minion nodes {plus} mana sustain. The finished tree comes together around maps.

*How to execute:* the in-game tree has *Ctrl{plus}F search* — type a notable’s name and it highlights with the shortest path. Or import Ghazzy’s PoB and follow it node-for-node. (A hand-written PoB URL can’t be trusted — the encoding is a node list.)

==== While leveling — priority order

Roughly one theme per act; take the cheapest route to each notable.

* *Acts 1–2* (~first 20 pts) — minion damage, head toward life: `Lord of the Dead`, `Death Attunement`, plus the small minion/int nodes between.
* *Acts 3–4* — your life {plus} mana: `Cruel Preparation` (life {plus} chaos res), `Discipline and Training`, a mana/ES node or two. Run Clarity lvl 1–3.
* *Acts 5–7* — more minion power {plus} life: `Grave Intentions` (minion chaos {plus} ele res), `Gravepact`, `Constitution`.
* *Acts 8–10* — aura reservation {plus} defense: `Sovereignty`, `Charisma` (reduced reservation — enables Determination {plus} Anger
** more), `Sanctuary` (spell block {plus} ES), `Puppet Master`.

That’s ~70–80 points and a functional mapping character.

==== Finished target (~115 pts, level ~90)

* *Minion damage / speed:* `Lord of the Dead`, `Death Attunement`, `Gravepact`, `Puppet Master`, `Redemption`, `Spiritual Command` (if pathing lands there).
* *Minion survivability:* `Grave Intentions`, `Indomitable Army`. (Keeping minions alive keeps Bone Barrier strong and cuts resummoning.)
* *Your life* (aim ~4.5–5.5k): `Cruel Preparation`, `Constitution`, `Discipline and Training`, `Written in Blood`, `Sanctity`.
* *Your defense:* `Sanctuary`, `Prism Guard` (spell block). The minion wall is your first defensive layer, block is your second.
* *Aura reservation:* `Sovereignty`, `Charisma`, one reservation-efficiency node.
* *Optional:* `Whispers of Doom` ({plus}1 curse — only if running two curses).

==== Jewel sockets (grab 4–5; ignore during campaign)

* *Regular jewels:* roll for `increased Minion Damage`, `Minion Attack and Cast Speed`, `increased maximum Life`, and the conditional `Minions deal % increased Damage if you've used a Minion Skill recently` (always true for you).
* *1 Large Cluster Jewel* (Minion Damage, 8 passives): *Renewal* (minions deal more damage while you have an Offering) {plus} *Feasting Fiends*.
* *1 Medium Cluster Jewel:* *Rotten Claws* (minion attack speed {plus} maim) or *Vicious Bite* (minion crit).

=== Endgame skill setup

*SRS 6-link (mapping):* SRS {plus} Minion Damage {plus} Multistrike {plus} Melee Physical Damage {plus} Elemental Damage with Attacks {plus} Feeding Frenzy.

* *Bossing swap:* Feeding Frenzy → Concentrated Effect or Predator. Keep a separate 1-link Feeding Frenzy on another minion (Carrion Golem or Holy Relic) so you don’t lose the buff.
* *Alternative:* transfigured *Summon Raging Spirit of Enormity* — fewer, bigger, longer-lasting spirits; more hands-off, great for bossing. Try base first, switch if you want less clicking.

*Auras (Necro runs a lot):* Determination {plus} *Anger* (flat fire to minions — staple) {plus} *Hatred* or *Pride* {plus} *Flesh and Stone*. Put *Generosity* on Anger (boosts it for minions, costs you nothing).

*Curse:* Flammability, ideally via *Blasphemy* (aura) or a Curse-on-Hit setup so it’s automatic.

*Utility bar:* Desecrate {plus} Flesh/Bone Offering, Convocation (pull {plus} heal minions), Flame Dash.

=== Standard budget upgrades, in priority order

[arabic]
. *6-link* for SRS — rare chest with life/minion mods, or start with a 6-link Tabula.
. *{plus}2 minion-gems Bone Helmet* with minion life (crafted or bought cheap).
. *Helmet enchant:* "`Summon Raging Spirit has {plus}1 to maximum`", or minion damage.
. *{plus}1 fire / {plus}1 all spell gems amulet*, then *Ashes of the Stars* (very affordable on Standard) — {plus}gem level and {plus}quality to everything.
. Rare wand crafted with *{plus}1 all spell gems / {plus}1 minion gems*, or a *Femurs of the Saints* staff ({plus}2 support gems, minion attack/cast speed) as a cheap midgame stick.
. *Watcher’s Eye* with an Anger or Determination mod; *Cluster Jewels* (Renewal, Feasting Fiends); rare crafted Bone Helmet.
